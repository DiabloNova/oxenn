## 1. Executive architecture reconciliation

The crosswalk keeps the two approved architecture layers separate. It does not treat the repository's older architecture documents as the new target contract. The target remains TypeScript-centered; SCRS concepts are selectively adapted rather than introducing a Python runtime or a fixed seven-stage reasoning graph.

### Product Architecture

1. Product Intelligence Profile
2. Digital Product Assessment — SEO / AEO / GEO
3. Product Value & Competitiveness
4. Geo-Market Intelligence
5. Product Evidence Graph
6. Simulation & ROI Lab
7. Expert Network
8. Planning & Execution Engine
9. Continuous Improvement Loop

Cross-cutting product capabilities: AI Copilot, Reporting, Workspace.

### Technical Intelligence Architecture

- Knowledge / Reasoning Graph

- LLM Provider Adapter
- Decision & Execution Engine
- Telemetry & Evaluation
- PostgreSQL / pgvector

### Governing invariants used in this matrix

- Evidence and provenance must travel with claims, observations, scores, and recommendations.
- Contracts must be versioned and validated at boundaries; metadata alone is not an authorization boundary.
- `UNKNOWN` is not zero, and missing evidence must not be converted into a measured negative.
- Provider execution mode must be explicit: `LIVE`, `MOCK`, or `HYBRID`; mock/demo data must not masquerade as tenant measurements.
- Human approval remains explicit for consequential decisions and execution steps.
- Product maturity must distinguish code presence, tested behavior, provider connectivity, persistence, authorization, and release readiness.
- PostgreSQL/pgvector is the durable persistence/evidence substrate; process-local maps are not a production source of truth.

## 2. MVP boundary and scope disposition

The current MVP priority is **Technical SEO Audit** and **AI Visibility Audit**. Findings affecting these flows, their evidence, tenant isolation, safe acquisition, provider modes, persistence, and truthful reporting are treated as MVP blockers or high risks.

Digital Twin, Geo-Market Intelligence, Simulation & ROI Lab, Expert Network, and broader planning/continuous-improvement lifecycles are future architecture coverage unless explicitly promoted into MVP scope. In particular, `RF-007`, `DOC-006`, and `SRC-024` are grouped as a future product-scope decision; their absence is not recorded as a current MVP bug. Public API, payments, PDF export, scheduled prompt execution, and some Workspace capabilities are marked scope-dependent where the approved MVP boundary does not establish them.

## 5. Priority semantics and evidence caveats

- `P1`: material security, tenant-isolation, data-integrity, or MVP correctness risk requiring resolution before relying on the affected MVP flow.
- `P2`: significant reliability, operational, test, or documentation foundation issue; sequence after P1 dependencies.
- `P3`: future-scope, policy-dependent, presentation, historical, or lower-impact maintenance item.
- No `P0` is assigned because the audits are static repository inspections and did not establish a live production exploit or outage. This does not reduce the seriousness of P1 security findings.
- The exact target files are proposed from audit evidence; final file scope must be reconfirmed in each numbered issue brief before implementation. Some evidence-only rows intentionally have no executable target file.
- No tests, scripts, SQL, database operations, migrations, builds, browser workflows, CI edits, or deployments were run during the audits or this crosswalk.
