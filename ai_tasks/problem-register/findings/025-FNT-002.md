# 25 — FNT-002: Font licensing and provenance records are incomplete

## Source
- Finding ID: `FNT-002`
- Original audit: `ai_tasks/font-folder-audit.md`
- Status: as recorded in the original folder audit; any conditional claims remain subject to revalidation

## Problem
The root font collection contains no README, license file, purchase record, source URL, or asset manifest. Box Face has embedded terms distinguishing personal and commercial use, requiring a commercial-use condition and accompanying license text; its web-embedding permission also requires confirmation. No license evidence was found in this folder for Yekan Bakh or Tajrid.

## Evidence
- `font/` has no standalone licensing/provenance documentation.
- Box Face embedded metadata states commercial-use conditions and distribution requirements.
- Yekan Bakh and Tajrid have no located license evidence in this folder; compressed WOFF licensing/name tables were not decoded.

## Affected files and paths
- `font/**`
- `public/fonts/**`
- Asset source records and deployment/distribution packaging.

## Impact
Shipping fonts without documented rights can create commercial and redistribution risk. The audit does not establish that the project lacks rights; it establishes that evidence is incomplete in the repository.

## Recommended solution
1. Obtain the original source URL, purchase/permission record, and license text for each family and variant.
2. Confirm web embedding, commercial use, modification, redistribution, and attribution requirements with the rights holder where unclear.
3. For Box Face, verify the commercial-use condition and the embedded `fsType` permissions; include required license text if the asset remains distributed.
4. For Yekan Bakh and Tajrid, obtain authoritative license documents rather than inferring rights from filenames or the font's presence.
5. Store provenance and license records adjacent to the asset manifest, with clear family/variant mapping.
6. Block production activation or redistribution of any font whose rights are unresolved; do not delete evidence or claim infringement without proof.

## Architecture alignment
Font assets are external licensed dependencies. Their provenance and permitted use must be explicit and reviewable before production distribution.

## Verification criteria
Each shipped font file maps to an authoritative license and permitted use; packaging includes required notices; unresolved rights prevent activation.
