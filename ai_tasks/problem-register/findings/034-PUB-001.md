# 034 — PUB-001: Provider logo strip is not mounted on the localized homepage

## Source
- Finding ID: `PUB-001`
- Original audit: `ai_tasks/public-folder-audit.md`
- Status: as recorded in the original public-folder audit; incomplete source-search limitations are preserved

## Problem
The provider SVG assets and BrandLogo consumer are connected, and PlatformsSection maps five available SVGs, but the localized homepage does not import or render PlatformsSection. The inspected page renders the landing header and footer without the provider trust strip. Repository-wide use search was incomplete, so this does not prove the component has no other consumer.

## Evidence
- `src/app/[locale]/page.tsx` imports/renders LandingHeader and LandingFooter but not PlatformsSection.
- PlatformsSection maps five SVG files under `public/logos/` through BrandLogo.
- Repository-wide dynamic-reference search was incomplete.

## Affected files and paths
- `src/app/[locale]/page.tsx`
- PlatformsSection and BrandLogo.
- `public/logos/**`
- Homepage information architecture and locale-specific styles.

## Impact
The homepage may omit a planned trust/coverage section, or the component may be retained without an intentional product role. The audit did not establish whether the section is still desired.

## Recommended solution
1. Confirm the provider strip's role in the approved homepage information architecture.
2. If approved, mount it at the intended location through the existing component and asset mapping; do not duplicate provider markup.
3. Verify Persian RTL and English LTR, responsive layout, accessible names, and correct provider/model labels.
4. If not approved, document the component/assets as intentionally unused and remove them only after complete reference checks and separate cleanup authorization.
5. Add a localized homepage regression test for the approved section state.

## Architecture alignment
The dashboard/site frontend must represent approved product capabilities accurately. A provider-logo strip is a presentation claim and must not imply integrations or live monitoring that are not actually available.

## Verification criteria
The section is either intentionally excluded with documented rationale or appears correctly in both locales and breakpoints without implying unsupported integrations.
